## Art direction

- Audience: first-time Azazel-Edge viewers with security/SOC familiarity but no knowledge of the product.

- One slide, one claim. Strong typographic scale, editorial space, sparse deliberate accents, no invented futuristic hardware, fake terminal output, generic neon UI, repeated uniform cards, or artificial security metrics.
- Visual principles: asymmetric information hierarchy, precise annotations, strong negative space, readable recording at 1080p.
